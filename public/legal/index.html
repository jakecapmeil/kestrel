Privacy

What we store. Your sign-in email and name, the profile details you give (birth year, sex, grade, height, weight, goals), your training log, plans, personal records, health and body-map notes, shoes, and your team memberships. If you connect a watch through intervals.icu, your intervals.icu key (encrypted), your runs and, when you open a run, its GPS route.

Who sees what. Only you (and your AI coach) see your health, body map, predictions, GPS routes and coach notes. Teammates see your name, colour, availability and RSVPs, and, unless you switch it off in You → Profile, your recent activities (distance, time, pace, heart rate and elevation) on the team's Activities tab. Your team roster shows name, jersey number, grade and events; your age is shown only to you, the team owner and team captains. Your weekly miles appear on the team mileage board unless you switch that off; team totals count everyone's miles without naming people. Team owners and captains do not get a view of anyone's training log or health.

Who processes it. Supabase handles sign-in, Cloudflare hosts the app and database, and Google (Gemini) or Cloudflare Workers AI answers when you use the free Kestrel AI. When you use your own AI provider, your messages go straight from your browser to that provider and are covered by their policy; your API key stays in your browser. We do not sell your data or show ads.

Kids and teens. Kestrel is for ages 13 and up and never asks for a home address or phone number. Parents and guardians can ask for an account to be deleted.

Your choices. You can change or delete your details at any time. In You → Settings, “Delete my Kestrel data” removes your account, log, plans, models and memberships. Backups age out within a short period. To ask us anything about your data, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

Health and safety

Kestrel's readiness scores, injury and illness flags and body map are prompts to take care, not diagnoses. If you are injured, sick, dizzy, have chest pain or trouble breathing, or pain that lasts or gets worse, stop and see a coach, parent or doctor. Kestrel does not give advice about medication, diet or weight loss. Heat and hills affect everyone differently: use the guidance as a rough guide only.

AI

Kestrel AI can be wrong. It may misread your data or write a plan that does not suit you. Check what it suggests, and trust your body, your coach and your doctor over it. Plan changes pass fixed safety limits (for example no big mileage jumps), but those limits cannot make a plan safe for every person. Do not put secrets or other people's private information into chats.

Team owners and captains are ordinary Kestrel users, not employees of Kestrel. A training plan they publish is theirs, not Kestrel's, and Kestrel does not check it for suitability. Athletes should follow their school or club coach for decisions about their team and can leave a team at any time. School and club leaders are responsible for their own duty of care, consent and any rules about collecting information from minors.
