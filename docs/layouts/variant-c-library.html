Variant C · the 5th tab is the library

Workouts get a tab of their own

The 5th tab is Library: the grid of workout boxes, now with Tools (calculators, training paces, taper) as boxes too, instead of being buried under Fitness. Using a workout swaps it straight into Today.

Run becomes Plan, Log, Fitness and Goals. Team is Feed, Calendar, Plan & groups (the coach's week plus who's in each group), and Meets & places.

Try it

  1. Library: filter by time, open Intervals, expand a workout, “Use today”.
  2. Library › Tools: try the heat calculator, or see your taper to League Champs.
  3. Team › Plan & groups: switch groups.
  4. Run › Log: tap days, page months.
  5. Today: everything without scrolling.

All three share Today, the profile picture for You, Record in the header, month calendars and the workout library. Fake data, no server; reload resets it.

Why it helps

  • The library and tools have room to grow without crowding Run.
  • Fitness gets shorter: calculators and paces move out.
  • Run and Team each have 4 segments.

What it costs

  • Browsing workouts is a separate trip from your plan.
  • Coach is only a floating button outside Today.
